# Dungeon interaction audit

Reviewed **24 November 2026** for ZKIR PARTY QUESTING **1.0** (previously numbered 1.2.0 during development). This is documentary research and a coverage audit. No live WoW client was available. Published IDs, quest text, player observations and historical scripts support the warnings; they do not prove current server behavior, credit, or complete coverage.

The audit found substantial gaps in development build 1.1.0: dungeon dialogue events, departures caused by hand-ins, and hazards that happen through objects, items, combat or proximity. The current 1.0 catalogue incorporates **17 additional rules**, bringing the full addon to **102 interactions: 77 acceptances, 19 dialogues and 6 turn-ins**. Of these, 59 have automatic quest-state evaluation and 43 require assessment of missing facts. These are rule counts, not counts of dungeons or independently verified event stages. Simulated verification and live-test limits are recorded below.

## Inventory and research method

The inventory covers **19 legacy dungeon complexes**, counting Blackrock Spire, Scarlet Monastery and Dire Maul once each, plus **nine new Forever dungeons**. The matrix splits Blackrock Spire into lower/upper, Scarlet Monastery into four wings, and Dire Maul into three, yielding **34 audit areas**. Upper Blackrock Spire is included for completeness; this addon supports solo play and parties of 2–5, not raid rosters.

For each area we compared the shipped rules with published quests, NPCs, objects and encounter descriptions. We followed references to consequential acceptance, reward, dialogue, release, summon and departure actions, and checked whether each occurs before or after the addon can observe it. Ordinary kill and delivery quests were not turned into hazardous acceptance rules. The [official new-dungeon inventory](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24301455/explore-the-new-dungeons-of-world-of-warcraft-forever), current [dungeon catalogue](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/dungeons-overview-locations-details) and [quest index](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/dungeons/every-dungeon-quest-location) supplied discovery lists, not proof that unlisted events do not exist.

Evidence is interpreted as follows:

- **Current game records:** Forever quest text and numeric NPC/item/object identities establish what an interaction refers to. Questie data supplies an additional metadata cross-check. Neither a quest's title nor its sharing flag proves what starts a server event.
- **Firsthand reports:** player descriptions identify timing and group failures. Forever pages also contain imported Classic, Vanilla and later-expansion comments; those are historical corroboration unless explicitly identified as current Forever observations.
- **Original encounter guides:** Method authors' descriptions and Wowhead encounter guides corroborate new dungeon behavior. Repeated copies on other sites are not counted as independent confirmations.
- **Historical emulator source:** CMaNGOS/vMaNGOS helps distinguish reward, acceptance, dialogue and object boundaries. It is not current Forever server code. No implementation was copied from it.
- **Official hotfixes:** the [rolling Forever hotfix article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-10-2026) was checked so old bug reports were not promoted into permanent eligibility rules. For example, the November 16 statue fix changes how old Sunken Temple wrong-order failures should be interpreted.

## New interaction rules

Opening the appropriate quest or NPC dialogue gives an opportunity to warn before the consequential selection. For an NPC-only rule, opening gossip does **not** prove the hazardous option is currently offered. The warning therefore names the action to wait before, and scene readiness remains Unknown until assessed.

## Corrections and unresolved evidence

**Acceptance minima conflict.** Current Forever quest listings show Willix **1144** at level **22** and Kernobee **2904** at **20**, while the inspected Questie raw data says **23** and **24** respectively. No authored correction in the inspected files settles the disagreement.
